Visit Borama

A welcoming city with beautiful greenery, a pleasant climate, and a vibrant culture.

Top three activities to do at Borama

Explore the green hills and countryside

Borama is a peaceful green city, known for its cool climate and scenic hills and valleys.

Experience the local culture

Visit local markets, Enjoy Somali dishes, fresh shaah, and the rich stories of Borama's history and culture.

Visit educational and historical areas

Walk around the Amoud University area, visit the older parts of town, and spend time in local community centers.
